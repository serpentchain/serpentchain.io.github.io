    Final Fantasy - GameBoy Edition

    Final Fantasy Classic for GameBoy - Cartridge Builder

    15 October 2026

    Initially released in December 1987 for the Nintendo Famicom (NES), Final Fantasy has been officially released for many systems but an actual port on the GameBoy never arrived... Until today! At the time of writing, this port is mostly code complete and is in need of play testing. The builder utility requires Windows and an active internet connection. This title is not for sale. It is available for free for those that own Final Fantasy (USA) for the NES. You will need to provide Final Fantasy (USA).NES in the builder utility. When generating the ROM, a unique ID, your WAN IP, and the current time and date will be permanently stamped into the game (this information is not collected or sent off to a 3rd party.) You must provide your legitimately sourced copy of Final Fantasy (USA) for the NES -serpentchain

    Sand Emperor MMC3 Romhack

    Sand Emperor MMC3 - IPS patch for demo5d9

    31 August 2026

    Sand Emperor, written by Ti, is a demake of Dune: The Battle for Arrakis (Sega Megadrive/Genesis) for the NES/Famicom/Dendy. I have created a memory mapper conversion romhack converting version demo5d9 from VRC6 to MMC3 mapper for a wider variety of flash cart support. I was motivated to do this hack simply by not having anything VRC6 capable to play on real hardware. VRC6 is an expanded audio mapper and therfore this conversion carries a sigificant downgrade in sound quality. Most of the effects are still there, but there will be more updates to follow to bring in better audio. This patch also delivers a single feature improvement: the units now blink when targetted. "Ya hya chouhada!"
